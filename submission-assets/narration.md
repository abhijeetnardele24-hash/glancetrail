# GlanceTrail — Voiceover Narration Script

**Total Duration:** 2 minutes 25 seconds  
**Speaker:** Clear, conversational, confident pace

---

[00:00 - 00:15]  
When you practice a presentation, notes help you remember your words—but they pull your eyes out of the room. GlanceTrail turns the room itself into a memory path so you can rehearse with your eyes up.

[00:15 - 00:35]  
Built for SPECS, GlanceTrail places three pitch beats directly in front of you. About one meter away, Problem sits on the left, Proof in the center, and Ask on the right. Only the active idea opens into a readable cue ribbon.

[00:35 - 00:55]  
During your first rehearsal, each beacon gives you a short cue, with deeper detail available on demand with a simple pinch. Advancing with the large Continue button folds the idea into a warm memory beacon and awakens the next beat.

[00:55 - 01:15]  
We advance through Proof and Ask. Once you finish Ask, the three locations connect into one visible trail, linking your ideas together in physical space.

[01:15 - 01:35]  
Now comes the transformation. On your second rehearsal, the sentences vanish. Only spatial symbols remain. If you hesitate, a quick pinch on the rescue hint reveals your cue for two seconds before fading away.

[01:35 - 01:50]  
If you turn away, an edge indicator gently points you back. You aren't reciting paragraphs—you're walking a path.

[01:50 - 02:10]  
Under the hood, GlanceTrail is built with the Spectacles Interaction Kit and UI Kit, using an explicit state controller with guarded inputs to prevent skipped beats. Six automated LEAF scenarios test every done criterion in preview—from one-active-cue constraints to hint timing and clean reset.

[02:10 - 02:25]  
This belongs on glasses because it keeps your hands free, your posture natural, and your eyes connected with your audience. GlanceTrail: Rehearse the path, not the paragraph.
