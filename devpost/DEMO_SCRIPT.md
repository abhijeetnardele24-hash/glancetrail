# GlanceTrail — Video Demo Script & Shot List

**Target Duration:** 2:25 (Mandatory limit: < 3:00)  
**Audio:** Original narration, clean spatial chimes, no background music  
**Visual Style:** Lens Studio Interactive Preview (1280×720 stereo view, additive milk-glass aesthetic)  
**Production Status:** Script and shot list ready; video recording deferred until user opens `glancetrail.esproj` in Lens Studio and approves the Interactive Preview.

---

## Shot List & Narration Timing

### Beat 1: Hook (0:00 – 0:15)
- **Visual:** Cut from a person looking down at notes or a smartphone screen to GlanceTrail’s three quiet memory beacons floating in the room.
- **On-Screen Action:** The camera centers on the 3 milk-glass beacons glowing softly at z = -110 cm.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"When you practice a presentation, notes help you remember your words—but they pull your eyes out of the room. GlanceTrail turns the room itself into a memory path so you can rehearse with your eyes up."*

---

### Beat 2: Product Promise & Orientation (0:15 – 0:35)
- **Visual:** Welcome card with tagline "Rehearse with your eyes up." User pinches "Begin rehearsal".
- **On-Screen Action:** Pinch cursor activates Begin rehearsal. The card fades, revealing the three beacons in a shallow 26° arc: Problem on the left, Proof in the center, and Ask on the right. Problem activates in glowing coral.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Built for SPECS, GlanceTrail places three pitch beats directly in front of you. About one meter away, Problem sits on the left, Proof in the center, and Ask on the right. Only the active idea opens into a readable cue ribbon."*

---

### Beat 3: The Guided Run (0:35 – 1:15)
- **Visual:** Guided rehearsal through Problem, Proof, and Ask.
- **On-Screen Action:**
  - Problem ribbon shows short cue: *"Screens pull your eyes away."* User pinches the ribbon to expand the detail: *"Notes help you remember words, but they break connection with the room."*
  - User pinches the large cobalt "Continue" button. Problem folds into a warm beacon; Proof awakens in champagne: *"One glance anchor per idea."*
  - User pinches Continue to advance to Ask: *"Practice the feeling of looking out."*
  - User pinches Continue. All three beacons connect with a luminous memory trail line.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"During your first rehearsal, each beacon gives you a short cue, with deeper detail available on demand with a simple pinch. Advancing with the large Continue button folds the idea into a warm memory beacon and awakens the next beat. Once you finish Ask, the three locations connect into one visible trail."*

---

### Beat 4: Signature Transformation — Symbol Mode (1:15 – 1:45)
- **Visual:** User clicks "Rehearse with symbols". All body text disappears.
- **On-Screen Action:**
  - Only the symbols (`[ ⌖ ]`, `[ ✦ ─ ✦ ─ ✦ ]`, `[ ☼ ]`) remain on the beacons.
  - User speaks through Problem with eyes up.
  - To demonstrate the failsafe, user pinches the circular "Rescue hint" button: the short cue temporarily fades in for two seconds with a warm halo, then self-hides.
  - User rotates view to simulate looking away: a soft edge arrow points left and one gentle guidance chime plays, disappearing upon looking back.
  - User pinches Continue through Proof and Ask to reach Final Completion: *"Path remembered. Now say it without reading it."*
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Now comes the transformation. On your second rehearsal, the sentences vanish. Only spatial symbols remain. If you hesitate, a quick pinch on the rescue hint reveals your cue for two seconds before fading away. If you turn away, an edge indicator gently points you back. You aren't reciting paragraphs—you're walking a path."*

---

### Beat 5: Technical Proof & Architecture (1:45 – 2:05)
- **Visual:** Quick overview of the approved `devpost/design-brief.md`, the SIK component hierarchy, and the passing controller unit test suite.
- **On-Screen Action:** `npm test` output showing all 6 criteria passing with green checkmarks.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Under the hood, GlanceTrail is built for the Spectacles Interaction Kit and UI Kit, using an explicit state controller with guarded inputs to prevent skipped beats. Six automated scenarios test every done criterion—from one-active-cue constraints to hint timing and clean reset."*

---

### Beat 6: Why Glasses & Closing (2:05 – 2:25)
- **Visual:** Return to the full stage view showing the clean reset back to Welcome.
- **On-Screen Action:** User pinches "Start over", returning cleanly to the pristine Welcome state with zero leftover artifacts.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"This belongs on glasses because it keeps your hands free, your posture natural, and your eyes connected with your audience. GlanceTrail: Rehearse the path, not the paragraph."*
